Answer only what you know. Unknown is a valid answer.

1. Which project must be easiest to find tomorrow morning?
2. Which repository, if any, is currently your main working repository?
3. Which project family should be analyzed first?

## Safety and privacy

4. Which repositories contain personal, legal, medical, financial, family, or identity-sensitive material?
5. Should the future control center itself remain public or become private?
6. Which wallet addresses and public identities may be included in public reports?

## Classification

7. May the inventory mark obvious empty repositories as `LEGACY_CANDIDATE`, without archiving them?
8. May likely duplicates be grouped as `DUPLICATE_CANDIDATE`, without merging or deleting them?
9. What does `canonical` mean to Jay: newest, most complete, deployed, legally authoritative, or manually selected?

## Daily workflow

10. What should the daily brief answer first?
    - What changed?
    - What is unfinished?
    - What failed?
    - What needs Jay's decision?
    - What should Jay work on today?

No automation receives authority from these answers unless Jay explicitly approves a later action.
