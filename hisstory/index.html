LETTER STATES

PTSD rating and Chapter 35 basis

The rating decision increases service-connected PTSD from 70% to 100% effective July 21, 2026. It establishes permanent and total status and basic eligibility for Dependents' Educational Assistance from that date. This is a finding about the veteran's qualifying status; each daughter's eligibility and application must be checked individually.

The notice says to file a dependency claim within one year of its date for possible retroactive dependent pay.

Decision packet: cover pp. 2, 5. Check each child's circumstances with VA.
